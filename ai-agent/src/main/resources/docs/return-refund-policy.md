# Acme Corp - Customer Return and Refund Policy

**Last Updated:** January 15, 2026  
**Document ID:** POL-REF-2026-01  

---

## 1. Overview
At Acme Corp, we strive to ensure 100% satisfaction with our products and services. If you are not entirely satisfied with your purchase, we are here to help. This policy outlines our standards for returns, refunds, exchanges, and warranty claims.

## 2. Standard Return Window
- **Physical Merchandise:** Customers may return unopened, undamaged items within **30 calendar days** of the delivery date for a full refund to the original payment method.
- **Opened Physical Items:** Items that have been opened but are in pristine condition with all original packaging, tags, and accessories may be returned within **14 calendar days** subject to a 10% restocking fee.
- **Digital Software / Subscriptions:** Software licenses and digital downloads are eligible for a full refund within **7 calendar days** of purchase, provided the activation license key has not been redeemed or utilized.

## 3. Non-Returnable Items
The following items cannot be returned or refunded:
1. Custom-configured or personalized enterprise hardware.
2. Gift cards and promotional promotional vouchers.
3. Downloadable software products with activated license certificates.
4. Clearance or "Final Sale" discounted merchandise.

## 4. Refund Processing Timeframes
- Once your return package is received and inspected at our fulfillment center, we will notify you via email of the approval or rejection of your refund.
- If approved, credit card refunds typically post to your statement within **3 to 5 business days**.
- Bank wire transfers and international transactions may require **7 to 10 business days**.
- Return shipping costs are free for defective items; for standard returns, a flat $5.99 return shipping label fee will be deducted from your total refund.

## 5. Defective or Damaged Products (Warranty)
- All Acme Corp hardware products include a **1-Year Limited Manufacturer Warranty** covering defects in materials and craftsmanship.
- If your product arrives damaged or malfunctions within 30 days, Acme will dispatch a replacement expedited unit at zero cost.

## 6. How to Initiate a Return
1. Contact customer support with your Order Number (e.g., `ORD-1042`) and Customer ID (e.g., `CUST-8812`).
2. Download and print the pre-paid RMA shipping label sent to your email.
3. Drop off the package at any authorized UPS or FedEx partner location.
