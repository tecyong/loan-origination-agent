# Acme Corp - Employee Handbook & Benefits Guide

**Effective Date:** 2026 Fiscal Year  
**Audience:** All Global Full-Time & Contract Employees  

---

## 1. Remote Work & Flexibility Policy
Acme Corp operates on a **Flexible Hybrid Model**. Employees are encouraged to work where they are most productive:
- **Core Collaboration Hours:** 10:00 AM to 3:00 PM in your local timezone.
- **Home Office Stipend:** Every new hire receives a one-time **$1,500 USD** tax-free equipment stipend for desk setup, ergonomic chair, and secondary monitors.
- **Monthly Internet & Connectivity:** Reimbursable up to **$80 USD/month** via the expensing portal.

## 2. Paid Time Off (PTO) & Leave
- **Flexible PTO:** Salaried employees enjoy discretionary paid time off with a recommended minimum of 20 days per calendar year.
- **Company Holidays:** 12 designated global company holidays plus 2 floating personal cultural observance days.
- **Parental Leave:** 16 weeks of 100% paid leave for all new parents (birth, adoption, or surrogacy), applicable within the first 12 months.
- **Wellness Days:** The third Friday of every quarter is a company-wide "Recharge Day" with no scheduled meetings or deliverables.

## 3. Professional Development & Learning
- Every full-time employee is allotted an annual **$2,500 USD** Learning & Development budget.
- Eligible expenses include technical conferences, professional certifications (e.g., AWS, Google Cloud, Spring Certified Professional), books, and online courses.

## 4. Health & Wellness Perks
- Comprehensive medical, dental, and vision insurance with 90% company premium contribution.
- $100/month fitness and mental wellness reimbursement (gym memberships, yoga studios, meditation apps).
- 24/7 Employee Assistance Program (EAP) providing free confidential counseling sessions.
